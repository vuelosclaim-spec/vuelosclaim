About us and sources

VuelosClaim is an independent project based in Valencia, Spain, that explains air passenger rights in plain language and calculates what you're owed. We are not an airline or a law firm.

How we calculate

We apply Regulation (EC) No 261/2004 and, for flights departing the UK, its UK version (UK261). We measure the great-circle distance between airports using OurAirports data and apply the €250, €400 and €600 tiers (£220, £350 and £520), the 50% reduction for flights over 3,500 km delayed 3 to 4 hours, the cancellation notice periods and EU Court of Justice case law on delays (Sturgeon) and airline staff strikes.

Sources

  • European Commission: air passenger rights
  • Regulation (EC) No 261/2004 on EUR-Lex
  • UK Civil Aviation Authority: delays and cancellations
  • AESA (Spanish aviation authority): passenger rights
  • OurAirports: airport data
