Privacy policy

The calculators run in your browser: your flight details are not sent to or stored on our servers. The details you type for the claim letter (name, booking reference, flight number and date, number of passengers) are only used in your browser to put the text together: they are not sent to anyone, not stored, and disappear when you close the page. We do not ask for bank details.

Cookies and measurement

Only if you accept in the cookie notice does the site load Google Analytics and the Travelpayouts affiliate script. Before you accept, or if you reject, they are not loaded and nothing is sent to them. With your permission we measure, in aggregate: visits, that the calculator was started and completed, the type of problem (delay, cancellation or overbooking), the result and the estimated amount band, opening, copying or downloading the letter, and clicks on the link to the claims service. We never send your name, booking reference, flight number or anything you type. Google signals and ad personalisation in Analytics are switched off. You can change your choice at any time with the “Cookies” link at the bottom of the page.

When you click an affiliate link, the claims service (for example, AirHelp, via Travelpayouts) may use cookies to record that you came from this site. The site shows Google AdSense ads: Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads, personalised or not depending on your choice in the consent notice. More at how Google uses cookies in advertising.
